FITMER

Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

The short version

FITMER is an interval workout timer. There are no accounts, no ads and no third-party tracking, and your data is never sold. The app does collect anonymous usage statistics and crash reports so we know what to fix — you can switch that off in Settings.

What we store

Your preferences (sounds, vibration, prep countdown, display options and similar settings) are saved locally on your device. Completed workouts go into a history kept on the device as well. Neither is uploaded to us.

Anonymous statistics

To see which features are used and where the app fails, the mobile app collects anonymous events (a workout started or finished, a setting changed) and crash reports through Google Firebase. They carry no name, email, location or advertising identifier, and they are not used to follow you across apps. Turn it off any time in Settings — Anonymous usage stats.

What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts or location, we do not use advertising identifiers and we do not track you across other apps and websites. The app contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

Permissions

On mobile the app may request to keep the screen awake and to vibrate during a workout, and plays audio cues. These are used only for the timer while you train and involve no data sharing.

On the web

The web version is served as a static site. Your settings are kept in your browser’s local storage on your device; nothing about your sessions is sent to or stored on a server by us.

Children

FITMER is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
